What's built, what's next
Working today
✓A live demo that triages a call and correctly routes it — oil delivery, billing, new customer, or general.
✓A full scheduling conversation that collects name, address, tank level, and timing, naturally.
✓Returning customers recognized automatically — tank size, delivery type, gate codes, account balance.
✓A draft-order view mapped directly to the fields on Droplet's own order screen.
Not yet built
×A live phone line — right now the demo runs as a web simulator, not a number someone can actually call.
×A real connection into Droplet — customer and order data are simulated, not pulled from or written to your system.
×Support for more than one dealer account at a time.
