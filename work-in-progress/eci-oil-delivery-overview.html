A real example
What this actually looks like
9:40pm, a Tuesday

Bob Henderson notices his tank's getting low and calls MyExpressOil. It's after hours — normally this would go to an answering service, or just wait until morning.

Instead, the call is answered immediately. Bob explains he's at about a quarter tank and would like a delivery sometime this week, mornings if possible. He doesn't give his account number or address — he's an existing customer, so the system already knows both.

By the time Bob hangs up, a complete order is sitting in Droplet — his name, account, address, tank level, timing, and even the note about his side gate code, all filled in. Nobody has to call him back to confirm the basics, and nobody has to type anything in the morning.
